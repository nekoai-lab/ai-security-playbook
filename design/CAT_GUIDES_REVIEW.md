## Effect and limits

Before: dense commands and paragraphs, with an easy-to-miss distinction below the first code examples. After: a clear introductory stopping point and illustrated note for that distinction, plus a light section break. The meaning remains available without images/JavaScript. This is an editorial and visual improvement; comprehension or completion gains have not been measured with readers.

Costs: extra image requests/downloads and vertical space, especially on mobile. Restrict to three intentional placements; do not repeat cats in every section or replace technical diagrams. Text-200% review covers enlargement at the specified widths, not every browser/assistive-technology combination. The five legacy-content migrations and pre-existing color warnings are separate remaining work.
